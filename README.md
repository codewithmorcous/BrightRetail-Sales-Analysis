# BrightRetail FY2025 Sales Analysis

I built this project to practice end-to-end data analysis in Excel. Took a full year of retail sales data (2,988 orders) and dug into what's actually happening with the business — who's buying, what's selling, where the money's coming from.

## Why I made this

I wanted a portfolio project that shows I can do more than just make pivot tables. So I picked a realistic retail dataset and went through the whole process — cleaning, analyzing, visualizing, and writing up what I found.

## The data

- 2,988 orders from Jan-Dec 2025
- 177 unique customers
- 5 regions, 7 product categories
- Columns: OrderID, Date, Customer info, Region, Category, Product, Quantity, Price, Discount, Total, Payment Method, Customer Type

## What I did

**Pivot Tables** — Built 6 of them:
- Monthly sales breakdown
- Region performance
- Category analysis
- Payment methods
- Top 10 products
- New vs returning customers

**KPIs I tracked:**
- Total revenue: ₹45.8L
- 2,988 orders from 177 customers
- Avg order value: ₹1,534
- Avg 16.9 orders per customer (that number surprised me)
- Discounts eat up 11.95% of revenue

**Charts** — 6 visualizations covering trends, comparisons, and splits.

## What I found

**December was huge.** ₹6.02L in revenue, way above February's low of ₹2.53L. Classic holiday season pattern.

**East region is our best market.** ₹9.95L, about 21.7% of total revenue.

**Electronics carries the business.** ₹17.15L — that's 37.4% of everything. If this category drops, we're in trouble.

**The customer loyalty is wild.** 177 people placed 2,988 orders. That's almost 17 orders per person per year. Returning customers make up 63.7% of revenue. The retention strategy is clearly working.

**We're too dependent on a few products.** Top 10 products = 88.6% of revenue. Smartwatch alone is 21.5%. That's a risk.

**UPI is winning.** Most used payment method at 728 orders. Cash is dying (only 436 orders).
